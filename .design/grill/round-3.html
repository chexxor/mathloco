Round 3 - gold demoted to accent: which primary takes its place

Your ruling: too much gold risks reading as Amazon/ecommerce, so gold becomes an accent and the primary changes. The measurement says your instinct is right but points at a different cause - and the fix is measurable.

Diagnosis, measured: the commerce read is saturation plus a complementary pair, not gold by itself

  • The loudest token on your site is not the gold. Chroma ranking of everything the build resolves: blue-600 C 0.245 (5 uses), amber C 0.165, blue-100 C 0.032, blue-50 C 0.014, gray-50 C 0.002. Two of five sit above a C 0.16 commerce budget, and they are the pair.
  • Those two sit 167° apart in hue - near-complementary blue-plus-orange at maximum chroma. That specific pairing is the conversion-optimisation palette of the web. Change the chroma and the pairing, and the commerce reading goes away even while the gold stays.
  • Per-page colour load today: index.html carries 6 painted surfaces of which 2 are chromatic; 404.html and chat/ carry 1 each. That is the baseline to beat, and it is a rule the phase-5 linter can enforce (chroma budget + chromatic fills per page), not a vibe.
  • Path hues constrain the primary. Four hues already carry meaning (calendar teal 190°, Meetup plum 305°, Discord rose 25°, Mobilizon grass 150°). A primary that sits near one of them makes the plurality picture unreadable - which eliminates three attractive candidates:
