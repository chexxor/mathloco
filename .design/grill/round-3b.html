The pattern. Brightness plus hue plus chroma is what carries the brand read. Hue at or below about 75 with chroma at or above about 0.15 and high lightness is the commerce badge zone - Amazon orange, Amazon yellow #FFB300, UPS gold, Walmart yellow, IKEA yellow, FedEx orange all sit in it, and so does the token this repo inherited from its 2024 template. Gold at L 55 C 0.12 hue 62 sits outside it - that is a gold used as ink and hairline, which is also the only gold that passes AA as text (4.81 on paper) and as a keyline (4.81 against the 3.0 non-text bar).

The honest trade: a bright gold fill can never be far from a commerce yellow - every value from L 76 to L 84 lands 0.07-0.08 from Amazon yellow, UPS gold, Walmart yellow, and also from UMN gold #FFCD0B and UCLA gold #FFD100. Lower the chroma and it stops being a badge: at C 0.06 the distance to Amazon orange roughly doubles to 0.120, and it still clears AA on a dark ground at 6.3-6.8.

If the dark changes, the path hues move with it

Those hues are ours, not the platforms' - they mean "this is the path", not "this is Discord". The rule is 40deg or more of separation between the primary and every path hue. Current set is calendar 190 / Meetup 305 / Discord 25 / Mobilizon 150. Against chalkboard (152) that collides; the set cyan 205, plum 315, coral 35, olive 105 separates by at least 47deg from chalkboard. Against sumi (60) the current set already clears 35deg or more, which is the one argument for B beyond taste.
