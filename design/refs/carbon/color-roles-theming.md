**Read against our system:** C1-col-01…04 and C1-col-03 are the same model our `@theme` block already
commits to (roles in one home, hexes only there). C1-col-06 is a divergence: we carry elevation as
binary (hairline/shadow) rather than as an alternating layer ladder. C1-col-08 is the one thing our
vocabulary has no suffix grammar for. C1-col-10 is a rule our `check:contrast` already holds.
