# Adobe Spectrum — iconography (domain 7)

Status: **ANSWERED with a size system and a stroke weight.** This is the one domain where this system
is measurably *ahead* of ours on numbers: a stroke weight (1.5 px), a canvas pair (20 px / 16 px) and a
drawn size (~18 px / ~14 px) are printed as prose, and they are the reason Medium weight exists.
