# Adobe Spectrum — navigation & wayfinding (domain 13)

Status: **PARTIAL — components answered, page-level wayfinding mostly not.** The site's
`/docs/pattern-library/left-sidenav` is a 16 B shell, so the pattern pages are out; what exists is the
component contract (side navigation, menu) plus the attention vocabulary that decides what a navigator
must compete with.
