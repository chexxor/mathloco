# Adobe Spectrum — typography & measure (domain 2)

Status: **PARTIAL — weights and sizes answered, measure absent.** The system's hierarchy model is the
most transferable sentence in this domain (it is the same reason our system keeps a 4.5 text / 3.0 display
bar), and the domain's *absence* is the finding: the type system names four things it defines, and line
length is not one of them.

| id | claim | kind | locator | quote | enforcement in source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| S2-type-03 | The one weight above that cap carries a size floor: Black only for headings at 18 px or larger, and only for product branding. | rule | `https://spectrum.adobe.com/foundations/typography/typography-system` | "Black should be reserved for heading type styles and used at 18 px or larger to maintain legibility." | none stated |
