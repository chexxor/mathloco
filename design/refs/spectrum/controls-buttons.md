# Adobe Spectrum — controls & buttons (domain 9)

Status: **ANSWERED, and answered in a way our system cannot be: the control is specified as a size
ladder plus a derived geometry rule.** The site's component pages are fragment stubs (`txt/spectrum-rsp-button.txt`
is 946 B of include URLs), so every claim here comes from the repo's markdown twin of the same page —
which is the coverage finding named in COVERAGE.md.
