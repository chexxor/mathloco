# Adobe Spectrum — feedback / notices & status (domain 11)

Status: **ANSWERED hardest of all — this system carries a five-variant semantic vocabulary with an
icon per variant and a stated reason for the icon.** The reason is always the same one: colour-vision
deficiency. That is the enforcement-free-but-reasoned half of our own "never convey meaning by colour
alone" question.
