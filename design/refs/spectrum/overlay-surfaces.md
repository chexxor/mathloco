# Adobe Spectrum — overlay surfaces (dialog, popover, menu, tooltip) (domain 14)

Status: **ANSWERED, and this is the densest domain in the corpus.** mathloco has no overlay today
(no modal, no popover, no tooltip in `src`), so rows from here will mostly land in the pre-loading
section of `GAP-ANALYSIS.md` — but the *constraint-aware placement* claims below are the transferable
ones, because they are the same problem our sticky footer and hero card have at small widths.

| id | claim | kind | locator | quote | enforcement in source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S14-overlay-01 | An overlay's size ladder is bounded by viewport: it scales down to 90% of the screen width, and height stays flexible. | rule | `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe/spectrum-design-data/main/docs/s2-docs/web/rsp/components/standard-dialog.md` | "Dialogs automatically scale down to 90% of the screen width on smaller viewports. Height is flexible and adjusts to fit the content." | the behaviour is declared in `packages/design-data/components/standard-dialog.json` (5,431 B, not opened); the 90% is prose |
| S14-overlay-02 | A dismissible dialog and a footer-action dialog are **different objects**, and the rule keeps them apart. | rule | `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe/spectrum-design-data/main/docs/s2-docs/web/rsp/components/standard-dialog.md` | "A dismissible dialog, which includes a close button, should not have buttons in the footer." | none stated |
| S14-overlay-03 | Whether the overlay can be dismissed by clicking the overlay itself depends on whether it has a close button — an explicit exception, not a general rule. | behavior | `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe/spectrum-design-data/main/docs/s2-docs/web/rsp/components/standard-dialog.md` | "Dialogs without a close button require one or more buttons in the footer to close the dialog or complete a task. The background overlay is not clickable." | none stated |
| S14-overlay-04 | An overlay is anchored to a source by construction, and dismissal is defined by the region outside it. | behavior | `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe/spectrum-design-data/main/docs/s2-docs/web/rsp/components/popover.md` | "Popovers stem off of action buttons that act as a source to open up transient content....A popover can be dismissed by clicking or tapping anywhere outside it, including the source" | none stated |
| S14-overlay-05 | When the source has no distinct down state, the connection is drawn with a tip instead — an if-then that solves an ambiguity, not a style. | rule | `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe/spectrum-design-data/main/docs/s2-docs/web/rsp/components/popover.md` | "When the source that triggers the popover does not have a visually distinct down state, use a popover with a tip to clearly indicate the connection to its source." | none stated |
| S14-overlay-06 | A small screen gets a **different component** (a tray), chosen by content volume — the responsive answer is a swap, not a shrink. | rule | `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe/spectrum-design-data/main/docs/s2-docs/web/rsp/components/popover.md` | "Trays can be used as alternatives to popovers on small screens. Use a tray when the amount of content is too large or overwhelming for a popover." | `packages/design-data/components/tray.json` (1,794 B) exists in the tree, not opened |
| S14-overlay-07 | Confinement is a declared mechanism: a container plus a padding value the surface must respect. | rule | `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe/spectrum-design-data/main/docs/s2-docs/web/rsp/components/tooltip.md` | "To make sure that the tooltip will stay within certain boundaries (e.g., a browser window) it's possible to define a container and a container padding value to respect." | declared as `containerPadding` in the component's options |
| S14-overlay-08 | Placement flips when space constrains it, and the flip rule is stated for both axes. | rule | `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe/spectrum-design-data/main/docs/s2-docs/web/rsp/components/tooltip.md` | "A tooltip placed at the top would flip to be placed at the bottom (and vice versa), and a tooltip placed at the left would flip to be placed at the right (and vice versa)." | declared as `shouldFlip`; the *why* is prose |
| S14-overlay-09 | An overlay's entrance is directional and its purpose is stated: to show its origin. | behavior | `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe/spectrum-design-data/main/docs/s2-docs/web/rsp/components/tooltip.md` | "A tooltip fades in and out when showing and hiding, and slides a short distance from the source to indicate its origin." | none stated |
| S14-overlay-10 | A long message is a **different component**, decided at a declared maximum width. | pattern | `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe/spectrum-design-data/main/docs/s2-docs/web/rsp/components/tooltip.md` | "A tooltip comes with a maximum width to disclose a short message. If a larger amount of text or information needs to be disclosed, Contextual help or Help text should be considered." | `maxWidth` is a declared option; the escalation rule is prose |
| S14-overlay-11 | **Absence:** no fetched overlay page states a maximum overlay height, a scroll behaviour inside an overlay, or a mobile-safe inset. | absence | `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adobe/spectrum-design-data/main/docs/s2-docs/web/rsp/components/standard-dialog.md` | "Dialogs automatically scale down to 90% of the screen width on smaller viewports." | nothing to enforce — the claim does not exist in this system |
