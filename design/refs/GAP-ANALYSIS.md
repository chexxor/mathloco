# GAP-ANALYSIS — five external systems read against our artifacts

**This is a plan, not a change.** Nothing in `design/refs/` touches `src/`, `DESIGN.md`,
`scripts/check-*.mjs`, or the decided-and-refused ledger. Every proposal here is a row with a detector
sketch, the defect its selftest would plant, and an evidence cost; nothing is wired, and no `D###`/`S###`
id is invented here (§5 names proposals as `DRAFT·<slug>` for exactly that reason — see `README.md`
hazard 2: `check:decisions` reads every tracked file, so a proposed id that parses like a real one turns
`verify` red on the branch).

Inputs, as read this pass: `DESIGN.md`, the `@theme` block in `src/styles/global.css`, and the printed
output of `node scripts/gate-run.mjs --list` (73 lines, 69 of them `check:` steps — @measured 2026-11-18
into `.design/tmp-refs/our-gates.txt`), plus `design/gate-cost.json` for the evidence costs.
Claim rows available for this analysis: 996 @measured 2026-11-18 by `.design/tmp-refs/verify-quotes.py`
run per system — govuk 191, carbon 195, atlassian 209, spectrum 186, material 215 — with 0 hard misses,
0 quotes over 40 words, 0 no-quote rows and 0 locators that are not a fetched URL, on all five.

**Verdict vocabulary, used and no other:** `MATCH` · `GAP` · `DIVERGENT-KEEP` (must cite a real `D###`
or `S###`) · `CONTRADICTED` · `N-A`. A gap for which no detector can be written demotes to
`DIVERGENT-KEEP` with a written reason, never to a silent `GAP`.

## §0 — Method, and the coverage ledger

**How "our state" is established.** Three artifacts only: a quoted line of `DESIGN.md`, a quoted token
or comment in the `@theme` block, or a quoted line of a gate's printed description from
`gate-run.mjs --list`. Where none of the three says anything about a domain, the row states
**SILENT** and carries the grep that establishes the silence, with its line count, so the reader can
re-run it. Silence is never inferred from the absence of a word in `DESIGN.md` alone.

**Corpus locators** are `<system>/<domain>.md#<row-id>`; every row cited here exists in that file and
carries its own URL locator and verbatim quote, which is what makes §2 quotable at the row-id level
rather than re-quoting 996 rows. `PRIOR.md` material was **not** used anywhere: it is marked
`UNGROUNDED — NOT ELIGIBLE FOR GAP ANALYSIS`.

### Enforcement ledger — where each system is behind our gate model

Our model is a **detector per rule**: `gate-run.mjs --list` prints 69 `check:` steps, and each
`check-<thing>` ships a `check-<thing>:selftest` that plants the defect it exists to catch
(`AGENTS.md`: *"A check without a selftest is an assertion, not a detector"*). Every system in the
corpus publishes rules with a mechanism that is not a build-failing check. That is the corpus's
cleanest evidence for keeping our enforcement model and importing their rules instead.

## §4 — What to steal exactly (MATCH)

Eight items, each named at the element or the word, because a paraphrase is where a rule goes wrong.
Stealing here means adopting their **contract**, never their values or their mechanism.

1. **The element contract for a key-plus-value row** — `govuk/data-display.md#G12-data-08/09`: a summary
   list *is* the description list, and the refusal names the alternatives **by element**. Ours is a `<ul>`
   of two-zone rows; the steal is the sentence "the choice of structure is checkable", which is what
   §2 rank 7 turns into a detector.
2. **Verb-first, object-named, punctuation-free labels** — `atlassian/controls-buttons.md#A09-button-03/04`,
   `govuk/controls-buttons.md#G9-btn-02`, `carbon/controls-buttons.md#C9-btn-02`. Adopt the grammar, not
   their examples; the detector is §2 rank 5.
3. **The route-integrity duty across media** — `govuk/navigation-wayfinding.md#G13-nav-12` +
   `govuk/loading-empty-error.md#G15-state-07`: every address, in letters and forms and on the site,
   points at a page that exists or redirects to one that does. §2 rank 1.
4. **The refusal of a disabled control, and the reason** — `atlassian/controls-buttons.md#A09-button-09`
   (twice), `govuk/controls-buttons.md#G9-btn-11` (the state it encodes is invisible to some users),
   `carbon/controls-buttons.md#C9-btn-10` (a temporarily disabled component never fully disappears, and
   an inline warning says how to re-enable it). Steal the *avoidance*; if we ever need the state,
   steal Carbon's half (keep its place, name the way back).
5. **Hidden text as the carrier of a translatable name** — `atlassian/accessibility-contract.md#A16-a11y-03`
   + `atlassian/i18n-rtl.md#A17-i18n-03`, with Carbon's `C16-a11y-08` (empty `alt` over
   `role="presentation"`) and GOV.UK's focusable variant (`G16-a11y-09`). §2 rank 9.
6. **Prefer wrapping over truncation** — `atlassian/data-display.md#A12-data-03`, Carbon's ellipsis-requires-an-overflow
   (`C13-nav-12`), Spectrum's conditional split (`S12-data-07`). §2 rank 16.
7. **A skip link is part of the navigation system, not an afterthought** — `atlassian/navigation-wayfinding.md#A13-nav-11/12`.
   §2 rank 11.
8. **An empty state explains what will be there, then gives one action** — `carbon/data-display.md#C12-data-07/08/09`
   (and the never-a-dead-end rule our exit band already enforces harder via `check:page-exit`). Ours already
   ships a status word, a `text-lede` title, a 48px primary **and** a second path; Carbon's one-action bound
   is the delta worth writing down, so the steal is the **bound**, not the shape.

make `check:decisions` red on a branch under review, which is the wrong price for a decision nobody has
ruled (§README hazard 2). **Priced** = what obeying it costs, and what reversing it costs. One refusal
below is declared **unpriceable** for an honest reason.
