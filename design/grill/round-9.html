Before any option: what the palette can even hold

Six hue families are already reserved — ink/rule/tint/band-text at 152°, gold/gold-on-ink at 62°/84°, and one hue per join path at 35°, 105°, 205°, 315°. One more arithmetic result, because it decides what a new token has to look like: for any colour whose luminance sits between paper and ink, its two contrasts multiply to the paper–ink pair ( 12.02). Asking for 4.5 text on paper AND 3.0 graphics on ink asks for 13.5 > 12.02 — impossible with one value. That is the proof behind every -on-ink twin, and behind MARK_COLOURS being keyed per ground.

Measured off this specimen (report 2026-11-02 09:55 — re-run the builder after a measurement round)

  • The shipped tints do not read as colour to the census: S-C paints 46.88% of its pixels at chroma 0.01–0.04 (the oklch(95% 0.03 …) fills) but only 0.5% at chroma ≥ 0.08, the level a reader would call colour. A wash is not area; if area is the goal, the tint has to go above ~0.04 or a darker field has to be used.
  • Spend it on meaning and the census sees it: hue families go 2 → 5 and the share a reader would call colour goes 0% → 0.4%, for a chromatic total that barely moves (13.6% → 12.7%) because the ink band dominates that count.
  • 1 planted pair(s) fail on the painted bytes: gold on tint at 4.39 (bar 4.5, S-C). Put a colour on a tinted field and it loses contrast it has on paper — gold is 4.82 on paper and 4.39 on tint.
  • Colouring the mark's two lines costs almost nothing in stroke survival — painted ink per box and the px at/over the 1.5 device-px floor are flat across the four options (on paper, px at 1.5 / ink pixels: K-A 3973px at 1.5 / 4522 ink, K-B 3930px at 1.5 / 4510 ink, K-C 3960px at 1.5 / 4508 ink, K-D 3950px at 1.5 / 4536 ink). The difference between them is in the look, not the legibility, so that part is a taste call the numbers cannot make for you.
  • The open-window accent is legal but tight: 4.56 as text on paper (bar 4.5) and 4.5 on the band — both clear by under 0.1, which is inside the noise of a font change. The warm candidate under R-4 sits higher (5.22 on paper) but costs a palette re-arrangement to exist.
  • Every one of the 32 colour tokens in the specimen renders within the same-colour threshold of its spec, so the numbers above describe the values in @theme, not an accident of the specimen.
