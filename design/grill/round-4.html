Two failures found and fixed while building this. The obvious rule colour at L 78-80 measures 1.9 and 1.79 against paper - a card edge that thin is decoration, not a boundary, so both themes now use a rule solved to exactly 3.0 (A: L 65.5, B: L 66; `.design/light-roles.mjs` solves for the most subtle lightness that still clears a bar). Second: gold text on a tinted chip measures 4.39 and 4.33 - just under AA - so gold never sits on a tint in this system: tints carry ink, gold carries paper. If a gold-on-tint is ever needed the value is L 54, not 55.

what this decides, and what is still open

Settled now: G1 - gold as ink, hairline and on-dark numerals, never a bright fill (oklch 55/.12/62 on paper at 4.81; 76/.06/84 on the band at 5.78-6.80); a rule solved to 3.0; paper oklch(98.5% 0.006 85); one dark band per screen, used for the durability line rather than for decoration.

The one question: A or B as ink. A carries a green undertone you can see in the large type and in the rules; B is warmer and about two steps quieter, and its contrast is higher (14.01 against A's 11.92). A came out of the club's own chalkboards; B came out of their near-black diagram ground. They are not far apart in price or in accessibility - this is a taste call, so look at the body copy and the nav, not the swatches. The path hues differ between them because of the 40-degree rule: A gets cyan/plum/coral/olive, B keeps teal/plum/rose/grass.

A real defect the specimen surfaced. The dates in the page payload are real (7, 12, 21, 26 November) but two of the four carry no usable time - one is 2026-11-12T00:00:00.000Z, which is a date with no clock attached, not midnight. So "no time set yet" is a state /schedule has to render on purpose rather than let default to 12:00 am. Row 2 shows it. That belongs in the round-5 pattern set.
