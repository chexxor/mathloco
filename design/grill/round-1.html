Round 1 of 5 — this is the only decision the code cannot answer

What kind of product is this?

Character sets the defaults for everything downstream: spacing density, type ratio, corner radius and motion speed — and therefore how the new surfaces the architecture creates (/schedule, an event page, the “Next: …” line in the hero) will feel. Pick one; nothing else in this round is ready until it is answered.

What the code does today (measured, so you can check every number against .design/drift.json):
  • 685 class writes, 186 distinct, in 17 markup files. Spacing: 10 distinct values; a 4px grid fits 9 of 9, an 8px grid only 6 of 9.
  • Type: 7 sizes. 1.200× fits 7 of 7 (moves 4.3px total), 1.250× fits 7 of 7 (5.9px), 1.125× fits 6 of 7 (11.1px), 1.333× fits 5 of 7.
  • Body text is already PT Serif (font-body 15 uses) — and only weight 400 ships, while the markup asks for 4 weights across 30 uses, so every semibold/extrabold in serif is synthesised.
  • Radius rounded-lg on 13 of 23 surfaces; shadow-lg 17 uses; duration-300 18 of 36 motion uses.
  • Accent is split: blue is painted on 5 different surfaces; amber is painted on exactly one place on the whole site — text selection (::selection), plus the share card. Measured contrast: white on that amber = 2.15 (fails); text-blue-600 on bg-blue-100 = 4.31 (fails AA); the green checkmark icons = 2.26 (below the 3.0 non-text floor).
  • A second, foreign design system is already in the build: prose prose-blue from @tailwindcss/typography, in one place (DiscordSummary.svelte:106).
