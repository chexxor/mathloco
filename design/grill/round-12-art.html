Round 12 — art for the photo slots, from public-domain books

Every image below is painted at exactly its slot size on --color-paper, cropped with subject-aware gravity, and measured off the crop's own bytes. The source is insideoutlibrary.com — illustrations cut out of public-domain books; 2,110,614 pictures from 59,407 books. Rights, quoted from the site's about page on 2026-11-03: "Every picture is checked against its library's rights statement before it is admitted (public domain, the Public Domain Mark, or no known copyright restrictions), so there is no permission to ask, no credit required, and commercial use is included." Nothing here is a photograph of this club — see the rule at the bottom.

The Byrne colour question, measured

Byrne's 1847 Euclid is the strongest content answer to the site's measured colour scarcity (the page paints 0.02% of itself at chroma ≥ .08 — colour a reader would call colour). But its paint is loud, and two of its hues sit on top of palette roles: its red-orange lands 0–3° from path-discord (35°) and its yellow 1–8° from gold-on-ink (84°), both at chroma 0.13–0.18, which is inside the zone that design/brand-distance.mjs puts next to Amazon/UPS/UMN gold. The knob is not a gentle fade. Between 50% and 25% saturation the plate's colour drops off the census bar entirely (9.0% → 0% and 3.7% → 0%), so anything above half strength still counts as colour. And desaturating moves the collision rather than removing it: 176-281's yellow sits at mean hue 81° at full strength and at 35° — exactly path-discord — at 50%, because lowering chroma moves the mean of a mixed stroke further than it moves the hue. Full-strength Byrne 176-281 measures 0.047 from Walmart yellow, which is inside the 0.05 "reads as the same colour" threshold design/brand-distance.mjs uses.

The rule this specimen does not get to break

These are illustrations from books, not images of the club. They may stand in the PS slots as artwork, and no copy may make them look like records of this club — D94 forbids stock people photography for exactly that reason, and the Meetup logo (design/records/0013-a-third-partys-mark-is-not-your-brand-colour.md) is what happens when borrowed colour becomes the most saturated thing on the page. The club's own shot list — the board after a meeting, venues, hands and materials — still stands, and PS-1/PS-2 are its slots. Strip EXIF/GPS on commit.

Measured back: the boxes in this page are the numbers in this page. node design/round12.mjs re-reads every box in Firefox and fails if a box is more than 1px off the slot it claims (the round-10 rule: a diagram that misrepresents the page is a diagram that lies).
