Two numbers that make the rules concrete

  • Fixed boxes, or the page moves. The one image on the landing page asks for 200×80 while its file is 834×799, and Tailwind's preflight sets img { height: auto } — so the rendered box is 200×192, and because it is lazy-loaded the page reflows by 112px when it decodes (measured: document height 2495 without it, 2607 with). Every photo slot gets a declared aspect ratio for this reason.
  • The loudest colour on the site is not ours. The Meetup png is 0.523% of the desktop page's pixels at hue 0-30° and its mean chroma (0.226) is the highest in the system (round 9's census). Dropping it takes the page's chromatic share from 3.77% to about 3.25% — which is the honest cost of the operator's Q4 ruling, and the reason area (photos, content) has to pay for the drabness instead.

Rules every slot inherits (already ruled, not new)

  • No stock people photography; no photo of this club exists, so strangers are a false statement about who attends (D86/D94). Prefer the board, venues, hands and materials.
  • An identifiable face is personal data: opt-in, first name by default, withdrawable (.notes/consent-and-data-policy.md rule 4).
  • Strip EXIF/GPS on commit — phone photos embed the location of a real gathering.
  • Never hot-link Meetup's CDN: their event images are theirs. Ask for source files (.notes/asset-sources.md).
  • Text on a photo takes its colour from the photo, not from a rule: the club's own four images split both ways (chalkboard mean luminance 0.014 takes light type; the parchment cover 0.791 takes dark).
  • Processing is already in the repo: astro:assets + imageService: 'compile' and sharp with gravity: 'attention' for subject-aware crops.
