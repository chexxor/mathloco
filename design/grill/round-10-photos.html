Two numbers that make the rules concrete

  • Fixed boxes, or the page moves. The image this page used to ship asked for 200×80 while its file was 834×799, and Tailwind's preflight sets img { height: auto } — so the rendered box was 200×192, and because it was lazy-loaded the page reflowed by 112px when it decoded (measured: document 2495px without it, 2607px with). It is out of the build now, which is why nothing in the media list is a photograph — but every photo slot still declares its aspect ratio for exactly this reason.
  • The loudest colour on the site was not ours. Before the 2026-11-02 mark demotion the survey found 0.48% of the desktop page at hue 0-30° with mean chroma 0.226 — the Meetup png — against 0.49% of the whole page at the "colour a reader would name" bar. On mobile it was 1.04 of 1.06. So ~96% of the site's visible colour belonged to another company. It is gone: the 0-30° band is now empty (the survey reports 3 families, none of them red), and the page's reader-named colour is 0.024%. The drabness is area and content, not another company's red — which is what these slots are for.

Rules every slot inherits (already ruled, not new)

  • No stock people photography; no photo of this club exists, so strangers are a false statement about who attends (D86/D94). Prefer the board, venues, hands and materials.
  • An identifiable face is personal data: opt-in, first name by default, withdrawable (.notes/consent-and-data-policy.md rule 4).
  • Strip EXIF/GPS on commit — phone photos embed the location of a real gathering.
  • Never hot-link Meetup's CDN: their event images are theirs. Ask for source files (.notes/asset-sources.md).
  • Text on a photo takes its colour from the photo, not from a rule: the club's own four images split both ways (chalkboard mean luminance 0.014 takes light type; the parchment cover 0.791 takes dark).
  • Processing is already in the repo: astro:assets + imageService: 'compile' and sharp with gravity: 'attention' for subject-aware crops.
