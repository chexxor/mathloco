I opened at the page that starts with the club's name, and I read it the way I read my calendar: find the next evening, the time next to it, the place after. For that one part my habits worked — there is a "next meeting" line with a day, a time written as a range, and an address, plus a small date up in the top bar telling me which day is up next without my having to count. Everything on that page that is *not* a date I could not use at all. The list of topic names, and the little pictures, are built out of words I have never met used this way, and nowhere on the page does it say, in plain words, what those words mean — so I cannot tell whether the evenings are for somebody like me or only for people who already speak that language. The page also keeps pointing me out to three separate places I use every day but have to already have accounts for: the place you sign up at, the chat, and the thing you add to your own calendar. When somebody sends me a date I add it to my calendar in one tap; here every sign-up seems to send me somewhere else instead. I then looked at the page that says the address could not be found, the chat page, the page that lists the dates, the join page, and a page about evenings that have already gone by. The dates page is the one that matters to me, and I read it with my normal habits: a big day number, then the time, then the place, the next one sitting at the top. But two things stopped me — the wording above the list says each kind of evening happens every week, while the list itself shows them only every other week, which is the kind of one-place-still-holding-the-old-date thing I am always the first to notice; and the whole list is stamped as "copied" from the club's own list on one single date, with nothing on the page to tell me it updates itself after that. The chat page gives me a number of "threads" but never a date for when that list was last looked at, and the names on it are not things I can open — in my chat a thread pulls off into a side-discussion I can click straight into, whereas here they are just lines of words. The page about finished evenings told me an evening was called off but then showed me nothing at all, which is the opposite of my calendar: my calendar keeps a called-off evening, greyed and with a line through it, so I can still see it was planned rather than that it never existed. I do not know what "today" is for these pages — everything on them is dated 2026 and I have no way to compare. I could not follow a link, open a thread, press a button, type or scroll anything, so everywhere that depends on what would happen next I have left it as a question rather than guess.
